Reading the web results

Intensity maps are normalized to the maximum of each section. Use the power chart to assess attenuation. Coefficient weights |c|² / Σ|c|² are not independent power fractions: the approximate vector basis is nonorthogonal and propagation includes cross terms.

Modes whose complex root does not converge are marked as approximations; their propagation constant uses the desktop model’s Marcatili estimate. Increase the grid and mode count to check convergence. The web simulation supports up to 128 physical modes and a 256 × 256 grid, subject to memory and spatial sampling checks. Pulse calculations, surface roughness and bends are not included.

Image comparison uses intensity similarity. Multiple modal mixtures can produce similar images; the coherent fit does not uniquely recover phase. HE/EH labels follow the source code’s Bessel-root convention; conventions in the cited papers can differ.
