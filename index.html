Reading the web results

Intensity maps are normalized to the maximum of each section. Use the power chart to assess attenuation. Coefficient weights |c|² / Σ|c|² are not independent power fractions: the approximate vector basis is nonorthogonal and propagation includes cross terms.

Modes whose complex root does not converge are marked as approximations; their propagation constant uses the desktop model’s Marcatili estimate. Increase the grid and mode count to check convergence. Automatic selection grows the basis to an input projection residual of at most 0.1%, subject to the available candidates, grid resolution and a 650 MiB working-memory estimate. This residual is not a bound on propagation error. Advanced settings also offers a manual mode limit. The grid supports up to 256 × 256 samples; the number of modes is limited by the actual profile memory and spatial sampling, rather than a fixed limit of 128. Pulse calculations, surface roughness and bends are not included.

Image comparison uses intensity similarity. Multiple modal mixtures can produce similar images; the coherent fit does not uniquely recover phase. HE/EH labels follow the source code’s Bessel-root convention; conventions in the cited papers can differ.
